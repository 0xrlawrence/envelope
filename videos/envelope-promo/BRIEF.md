## Intent

Create a fast-paced modern motion-graphics promotion for Envelope. Start with
the live app, turn its paper envelope into a flying paper plane, and explain the
complete STRK flow clearly enough that a new viewer understands both normal and
shielded funding, contract parking, bearer-link claims, and the one-to-one
shielded balance outcome. The tone is cinematic, confident, technical, and
compact rather than playful or hype-heavy.

## Assets

- https://0xrlawrence.github.io/envelope/ — canonical live product and interface source.
- Existing Envelope repository artwork and UI assets — envelope, paper plane, security patterns, token marks, and product branding.

## Customizations

- Zoom into real app screens and interaction details.
- Animate an envelope folding into a paper plane and flying through the system.
- Explain the shielded and normal funding paths, including both claim outcomes.
- Use fast electronic background music, paper-fold sounds, whooshes, UI clicks, cryptographic pulses, and an impact-led logo finish.
- Add concise cinematic English voiceover and readable embedded captions.

## Notes

- Landscape 16:9 at 1920x1080, targeted at 45 seconds.
- Storyboard review is required before final production.
- Automatic production means the agent makes creative and technical choices after each required approval gate.
- Preserve the app's dark postal-security visual language: cream paper, navy security lines, Starknet blue, and orange accents.
- Do not imply that public STRK becomes a different wrapped token. The same ERC-20 STRK moves one-to-one into the privacy pool when claimed privately; no minting or value creation occurs.
- End card should include the Envelope name, product URL, and @0xrlawrence.
